# Scenario: First Lesson Handoff

User asks to start a practice lesson.

Good response shape:

- Names the goal and why it matters.
- Gives files, constraints, success criteria, and one hint.
- Asks the learner to attempt the core work first.
- Does not dump the finished answer.

Bad response shape:

- Solves the whole exercise immediately.
- Turns the handoff into a lecture.
- Leaves the learner without a concrete next action.
